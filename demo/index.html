MapLibre GL 6.x (loaded by MCO.map.loadMapLibre()) with cartoStyleUrl(), house navigation + fit-to-extent controls, the zoom floor, and the Montana boundary overlays painted by overlayPaints(). The legend is an .mco-panel wired with MCO.initCollapsible — on a compact viewport it starts collapsed. Camera and theme round-trip through the URL.

Drawer & sheet

MCO.initDrawer: a labelled <aside> that makes the rest of the page inert while open (Tab can only cycle inside), closes on Esc / × / scrim, and returns focus. MCO.initSheet: docks at the end edge here, at the bottom with peek/full detents on a phone. The grip is the keyboard twin of the drag. Both sit on MCO.overlay.

Tooltip

The pointer-following .mco-tooltip. It is aria-hidden — hover-only content must also reach assistive tech another way (live region, popup, or table twin).

What this page is

A living exercise of every component in the kit — tokens, navbar, buttons, toast, tooltip, modal, collapsible panel, and the MapLibre helpers. It doubles as the target for the automated axe accessibility audit in CI.

Try

  • Press Tab from the top of the page — the skip link appears first.
  • Close this dialog with Esc — focus returns to the button that opened it.
  • Zoom the map out — it snaps back to Montana.
  • Switch to High contrast and watch every surface re-derive.
