Map category palettes: proposal

On the map tools, orange and brown meant something different in each tool, and all of it sat on the kit's brown tribal-lands fill. This page puts each tool's category set as it is on main today (Before) next to the proposed kit set from MCO.palette.categorySet() (After), in all three themes. Each set is shown as legend swatches and as marks over basemap land, a reservation (the tribal fill and its line) and a lake, for normal vision and simulated protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia.

The reserved band is OKLCH hue . The figure after each swatch is its lowest contrast against --bg-deep, --bg-surface, basemap land, water and the tribal fill (cells: the fill at its opacity over land). A figure in red is under 3:1, and reserved marks a hue inside the band. node tools/check-contrast.mjs --report prints every ratio and the closest pair for each vision type.
